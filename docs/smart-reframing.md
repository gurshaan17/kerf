# Smart reframing

Select a video clip on the timeline and choose **Reframe video** in the
Inspector, or **Reframe selected video** in the Assistant panel. Choose
**Vertical 9:16** or **Square 1:1**. Click a subject on the
source image, or use the horizontal and vertical focus sliders. The outline
shows which part of the source fills the new frame.

Use the preview scrubber to review the framing and place corrections at specific
times. **Track subject** follows the selected image region between corrections.
If Kerf loses the subject or detects a new shot, it holds the last usable
position and marks the interval. Jump to a marked interval, choose the subject
again, and rerun tracking if the subject moves. The preview shows safe zones.

Choose **Create vertical project** or **Create square project** to save an
independent, editable timeline. Open the new version to adjust its transform
keyframes, add captions and titles, or export. The new project keeps the
matching source audio and points to the original media file.

The first version supports one ready, normal-speed video without existing
visual effects or overlapping layers, plus its matching audio. Review up to
two minutes at a time. Tracking runs locally and follows visible image detail.
Rapid movement, occlusion, similar subjects, and lighting changes can require
manual corrections.
